## 1. Cookies {#cookies}

sitehp.app sets no cookies. It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it loads no fonts, scripts or images from other companies’ servers.

## 2. Browser storage {#storage}

sitehp.app keeps two small items in your browser, and nothing else:

- `sitehp-theme` in local storage (localStorage): `dark` or `light`, set only when you press the theme button, kept until you delete it.
- `tsr-scroll-restoration-v1_3` in session storage (sessionStorage): how far you had scrolled on the pages you visited, so Back returns you to the same place. It is strictly necessary for the navigation and is cleared when you close the tab.

## 3. Why no consent banner {#consent}

The theme only remembers a choice you made yourself, and the scroll positions are strictly necessary for moving back and forth. Neither is sent anywhere, identifies you or is used for tracking, so the law does not require your consent and the site shows no banner.

## 4. Cookies of the sites you scan {#scanned-sites}

Scanning a website does not put that website’s cookies in your browser: the sitehp.app server makes the requests, not you. The scanner reads the cookies a scanned site sends, to check their security flags, but never stores their values.

## 5. How to delete it {#delete}

Delete the site data of sitehp.app in your browser settings. The site then starts again in its default dark theme. The scroll positions go by themselves when you close the tab.
