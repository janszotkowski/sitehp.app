## 2. The service {#service}

sitehp.app checks a public website from the outside, the way a visitor, a search engine or an AI agent sees it, and gives it a score from 0 to 100 with a list of what to fix first. It is free and needs no account. How the score is calculated is described on the [How scoring works](/en/methodology) page.

## 3. Fair use {#acceptable-use}

You may scan any publicly reachable website. You may not:

- use the service to attack, overload or harm a website, including by starting scans in bulk or by script,
- get around the limits on how many scans you can start, for example by changing addresses,
- try to make the scanner reach addresses that are not public, or test or attack the service itself without my permission,
- present the results in a misleading way, for example as a certified security audit.

## 4. Limits and blocking {#enforcement}

The service limits how many scans one address can start and how often one domain can be scanned. I may refuse scans of some domains, block addresses that misuse the service, and limit or stop the service at any time, without notice where misuse requires it.

## 5. Reports and sharing {#reports}

Every scan has its own link. Anyone who has the link can see the report, so share it only with people who should. Reports are kept out of search engines and deleted after 30 days. The same address scanned again within a few minutes may be answered with the report already made.

Values in the query part of the address are hidden before a report is stored, but the rest of the address is kept as entered: do not scan addresses that contain secrets in their path.

You may share a report and quote its results, as long as you do not change them.

## 6. What a score means {#results}

A scan is an automated check of what is visible from the outside, at one moment. It is not a penetration test, a security audit, legal advice or a guarantee: a site can have problems a scan cannot see, and a finding can be wrong because of a firewall, a cache or a temporary fault. The score follows my own published model, which may change; scores are comparable only within one model version.

Decide about changes to your site on your own judgement, ideally with the person who runs it.
