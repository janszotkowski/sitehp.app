## 1. My aim {#commitment}

I want everyone to be able to use sitehp.app, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard only, magnification or reduced motion. I aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## 2. How far I am {#status}

sitehp.app partly meets WCAG 2.2 AA. What is in place:

- every page and every control works with a keyboard, with a visible focus ring and a link to skip to the content,
- a page change and the progress and outcome of a scan are announced to screen readers,
- the score rings and other graphics have text alternatives, and tables have proper headers,
- text and controls reach the required contrast in both the dark and the light theme, checked automatically on every change,
- buttons and the scan field have a touch target of at least 44 pixels; smaller targets, such as the footer links, are at least 32 pixels, above the 24-pixel minimum of WCAG 2.2 AA,
- animations stop when your system asks for reduced motion,
- the layout adapts to narrow screens, down to a phone.

## 3. Known limits {#limits}

- The site has not yet been tested end to end by people who rely on a screen reader. I plan to do it before the public launch.
- The report table holds dozens of findings and can be long to move through with a screen reader; the area filter and the search help to narrow it.
- The site is available only in English and Czech.

## 4. How I test {#testing}

Automated tests check the contrast of every text and control in both themes and the keyboard and focus behaviour of the report, the menus and the dialogs. I also go through new pages by hand with a keyboard.

## 5. Tell me what does not work {#feedback}

If something on sitehp.app is hard or impossible to use for you, write to me on {{contact}}. Tell me the page and what happened; I reply within two working days and, where I can, send you the information in a form that works for you.

## 6. About this statement {#legal-basis}

This statement is voluntary: the Czech accessibility law (Act No. 99/2019 Coll.) applies to public bodies, and sitehp.app is a free service run by a private individual. I prepared it myself and update it when the site changes.
